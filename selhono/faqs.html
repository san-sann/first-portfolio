FAQ’s

Every Question Answered

What is the Selhono Interior Design Service?

Selhono pairs you with a dedicated interior designer who plans the layout, sources furnishings and manages the full renovation from concept to installation, so you get a finished space without juggling contractors yourself.

So, how exactly does this work?

After a short discovery call we build a 3D concept and moodboard for your space, refine it together, then order and install everything on your behalf — you approve every stage before we move forward.

What cities do you currently operate in?

We currently take on projects across major cities in Australia and the US, with remote design consultations available anywhere in the world.

Do you offer virtual design consultations?

Yes — if you're outside our service area or simply prefer to work remotely, our designers can plan your space entirely over video calls and shared moodboards.

What kind of interior designers do you have?

Our team is made up of licensed, experienced designers who specialise in a range of styles, from minimalist and Scandinavian to warm, eclectic interiors, and we match you with the designer whose portfolio fits your taste.

Project related questions

How long does it take?

Most single-room projects are completed within four to six weeks of final approval, while full-home renovations typically take two to four months depending on scope.

Can I use my existing furnishings?

Absolutely — tell us which pieces you'd like to keep, and your designer will build the new plan around them, mixing in new furnishings only where they're needed.

How much time do I need to put into redesigning my interior?

We handle sourcing, ordering and installation, so most clients spend just a couple of hours a week reviewing moodboards and approving selections.

What do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the size of your space and the scope of work — after your free consultation we'll provide a detailed quote with no hidden fees.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes — every project starts with a complimentary consultation so we can understand your space, your style and your budget before any commitment.
