Frequently asked questions

How do I reset my password?

Open the Log In page and select “Forgot password?” below the sign-in form. You’ll be asked to confirm your login and set a new password.

How do I submit my weekly timesheet?

Go to Timesheet , fill in the hours for each project on your current week, and confirm before the Friday deadline. Your manager is notified automatically once the week is submitted.

How do I request vacation days?

Open Vacations from the account menu and add a new entry to the Planned vacations table. Requests are reviewed by your division manager and reflected in your Actual vacations balance once approved.

How do I register for a training session?

Browse upcoming sessions under Training — Find training , then confirm your seat on the Training registration page. Completed sessions appear in your Corporate CV.

How do I update my personal details?

Open My details from the account menu and select the edit icon next to your name to update contact information and branch assignment.

Who can access the Administration panel?

Only users with the Administrator role can manage accounts and permissions on the Administration page. Contact support below if you need your access level changed.
